Privacy Policy for SurfNClean

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Dzidzo Games ("we", "us", "our") built the SurfNClean mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play SurfNClean, and how it is used.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

SurfNClean does not require an account, login, email address, or any personal registration. Your game progress (coins, unlocked characters/boards, settings, achievements) is stored only locally on your device and is never sent to us. We have no server that stores your saved game.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

SurfNClean uses the following third-party services to show ads and keep the game free. These providers may automatically collect information such as your advertising ID, device type, general location (country/region), and app usage data, in order to serve and measure ads:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services (for distribution on Google Play) — Google Privacy Policy
  • TapTap (where distributed via the TapTap store) — see TapTap's own privacy policy on their platform

We do not control how these third parties process data. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies directly. On devices/regions where required, you will be shown a consent prompt (Google's User Messaging Platform) before any personalized ads are shown, and you can change your ad preference at any time from your device's ad settings.

3. Local Notifications

If you enable notifications, SurfNClean schedules simple local reminders directly on your device (e.g., "come back and play"). These are generated and scheduled entirely on-device — no data about your play habits is sent to us or anyone else for this purpose. You can turn this off anytime in Settings.

4. Children's Privacy

SurfNClean is designed to be family-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the game may be installed by a general audience, including children, ads are configured to request non-personalized/child-appropriate ad settings where required by the app store (e.g., Google Play Families policies, COPPA). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it — though as noted above, we do not operate any system that collects or stores personal information in the first place.

5. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect or store personal data ourselves, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, or delete. For data collected by Google AdMob or Google Play (such as your advertising ID), you can exercise your rights (access, deletion, opt-out of personalized ads) directly through your device's privacy settings or Google's own account tools.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
